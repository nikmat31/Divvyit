Bill splitting just got easier. Introducing Divvy!

A simple way to split any food bill with friends.

Split a bill →

No login needed.

Everyone pays for what they actually ate

Photograph the bill and Divvy reads every line, so all you do is tap who had what. Each dish splits only between the people who ordered it — tax and service included.

Then it goes in the group chat

Divvy turns the split into a receipt with everyone's name and number, rounded off and still adding up to exactly what you were charged. Send it to the group, save the image, or copy it as text.
