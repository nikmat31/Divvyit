Open the link and start. Your bills live in your browser — there's no database, no profile, nothing to delete later.

🧾

Reads the messy ones

Crumpled thermal receipts, angled photos, dark-mode screenshots. It crops to the bill, reads the columns, and keeps the quantities.

🧮

Maths you can check

Tax and tip are split by what each person actually ate. Divvy compares its total against the one printed on the bill and tells you when they disagree.

💬

Ends in the group chat

Whole-number amounts that still add up exactly, on a receipt image you can send anywhere. No "settle up" nagging.

Nobody subsidises the drinkers

Even splits are the reason bill-splitting feels unfair. Divvy divides each dish only between the people who had it — and the −/+ buttons handle the plate of three where one person had two.

Tax and service then spread in proportion to what each person ate, so the person who had a soda isn't covering someone else's whisky.
