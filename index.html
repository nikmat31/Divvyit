No login needed.

The bill arrives and the table goes quiet. Fourteen items, someone who only had a starter, someone who ordered all the beer, and tax spread across the lot of it.

Divvy handles it in about a minute. Take a photo and it reads the bill for you — every dish, quantity and price, tax lines included. Then you just tap the names of whoever had what. No typing out items, no spreadsheet, no doing sums at the table while everyone waits.

Everyone pays for what they actually ate

This is the bit that usually causes the argument. Splitting evenly is easy maths and quietly unfair — the friend who had a lime soda ends up covering someone else's third whisky.

Divvy divides each dish only between the people who ordered it. Two beers instead of one? Tap twice. Tax and service follow the same logic, spread in proportion to what each person actually had, so the numbers land where they should.

Then it goes in the group chat

When you're done, Divvy turns the split into a clean receipt — everyone's name, everyone's number, rounded off and still adding up to exactly what the restaurant charged.

Send it straight into the group, save the image, or copy it as text. Nobody needs to install anything to read it, and nobody gets a "settle up" reminder three weeks later.
